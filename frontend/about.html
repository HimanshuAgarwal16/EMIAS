ABOUT EMIAS

Connected information for critical moments.

EMIAS is a secure healthcare information framework designed to connect patient medical information with authorized hospital access, while providing AI-assisted analysis of supported medical reports.

⌁
Patient • Hospital • Information • Care
WHY EMIAS

Designed around the information gap.

During an emergency, important medical history may need to be available quickly. EMIAS brings identity, medical information and controlled healthcare access into one connected framework.

The challenge

  • Medical history may not be immediately available.
  • Allergies, medications and chronic conditions can be important during urgent care.
  • Different information sources can make retrieval slower and less organized.

The EMIAS approach

A unique EMIAS ID connects a patient's registered information with authorized hospital access. Security controls, access logging and AI-assisted report analysis are designed as supporting layers around the core information system.

THE EMIAS JOURNEY

From identity to informed care.

The system is organized as a connected flow rather than a collection of isolated features.

01

Register

Patient information is collected and a unique EMIAS identity is created.

02

Secure

Medical information is organized with authentication and controlled access.

03

Retrieve

Authorized hospital users can locate a patient using the EMIAS ID.

04

Assist

Supported reports can receive AI-assisted analysis for clinical review.
